Welcome to the Snake Game

Welcome to the classic arcade playground! Take a break, grab a hot drink, and test your reflexes. Whether you are looking for a quick retro nostalgia fix or a strategic path to master every challenge, you have come to the right place.

How to Play

  • Use Arrow Keys (or WASD) to slither in four directions.
  • Munch on tasty red apples to grow your tail and rack up high scores.
  • Watch out for boundary walls, inner obstacles, and your own tail!
  • Press Spacebar whenever you need to pause the action.

Choose Your Journey

How would you like to play today? Dive into a campaign featuring 10 levels that build on one another, each with unique obstacle maps, or play an endless game with custom speed settings.

Level Mode

Have fun trying out the different levels! Can you make it all the way to the end level?
