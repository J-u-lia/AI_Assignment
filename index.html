Welcome to the Snake Game

Ready to play? Guide your snake, collect the food, and try to beat your high score!

How to Play

  • Use Arrow Keys (or WASD) to slither in four directions.
  • Eat the tasty red apples to grow your tail and rack up high scores.
  • Watch out for boundary walls, inner obstacles, and your own tail!
  • Press Spacebar whenever you need to pause the action.

Choose Your Journey

Choose how you want to play! Take on 10 levels or test your skills with different difficulty modes.
